Key Security & Privacy Features

All-in-one protection designed for digital life and modern device fleets.

Real-Time Threat Protection

Advanced multi-layered security with artificial intelligence helps defend your device against existing and emerging online threats, viruses, ransomware, and spyware.

Secure VPN (Virtual Private Network)

Browse anonymously and securely with a no-log VPN that adds bank-grade encryption to protect your sensitive data on public Wi-Fi hotspots.

Smart Firewall

Monitors network traffic between your computer and other devices, blocking unauthorized incoming and outgoing communication to safeguard private files.

Integrated Password Manager

Generate, store, and manage complex passwords, credit card credentials, and other personal logins securely in an encrypted cloud vault.

PC Cloud Backup

Automatic, preventive cloud storage to back up important files, photos, and financial documents to prevent data loss due to hard drive failures or ransomware.

Dark Web Monitoring

Continuously patrols the dark web and notified forums to alert you if your personal credentials, email addresses, or account details are exposed.
